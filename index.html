Our Philosophy

Cooked slowly.
Served generously.

At Rashko, every dish begins long before the table is set — with marinades that rest overnight, lamb that braises until it surrenders to the bone, and rice perfumed by saffron grown under desert sun.

Our recipes travel the old caravan routes from Sana'a to Damascus, but the hospitality is purely ours: warm, unhurried, and deeply Northern Nigerian in spirit.
