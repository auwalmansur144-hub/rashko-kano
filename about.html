Our Story

A House of Hospitality

“Ahlan wa sahlan — you are family, and the table is yours.”

Rashko opened its doors on Gwarzo Road with a single ambition: to bring the unhurried rhythm of an Arabian kitchen to Kano. Our chefs trained between Aden, Damascus and Riyadh, and they cook the way their grandmothers did — with patience, generosity, and respect for the season.

The lamb is locally sourced and slow-roasted overnight. The bread is pulled from a wood-fired oven in front of you. The spices are blended in-house, ground fresh each morning.

We are a small room of brass lanterns, terracotta walls, and arched windows — but our welcome is wide. Whether you arrive for a quiet lunch, a long celebration, or a mansaf shared between friends, you'll leave fed, rested, and a little spoiled.
